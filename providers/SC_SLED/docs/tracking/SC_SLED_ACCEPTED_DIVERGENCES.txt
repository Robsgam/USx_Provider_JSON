DriverLicenseQuery | QWDQ | ImageIndicator | devdoc-optional-unreachable | Metadata is FIELD authority and QWDQ does not define ImageIndicator. The raw <Requirements> for DriverLicenseQuery keyReference=QWDQ (the sanctioned raw-XML exception) reads Set[SexCode,BirthDate,Name] with Any[OperatorLicenseNumber,State,State2..State5] -- no ImageIndicator. ONLY the sibling DQ variant defines it (Any[ImageIndicator,State..5]). The devdoc lists ImageIndicator as an optional of DriverLicenseQuery FLAT across both variants, which is the known structural ambiguity: the devdoc gives one optional list per query while metadata scopes optionals PER VARIANT.
