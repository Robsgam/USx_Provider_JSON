NOT a build defect and NOT an oversight -- a measured platform trade. Query checkboxes are keyed by ENTITY (proven by CHECKBOX_PROBE on usx-sc-sled: two forms sharing one entity showed all four QIDMs on BOTH tabs), and the entity set is capped at SIX by the client itself (Sm = Object.values(ut) over PERSON/VEHICLE/FIREARM/ARTICLE/BOAT/OTHER). SC_SLED needs seven forms. A seventh form must double an entity, and a doubled entity ALWAYS strands a permanently-dead checkbox on both of its tabs. So the choice was seven tabs carrying dead checkboxes or six clean tabs minus one form. AM is the one dropped: it is the only NON-SEARCH transaction SC declares Basic, and its tab is where the stray Boat checkbox that started this appeared. TO RESTORE: give AM targetEntity=Other and move WantedPersonQuery back to Firearm -- that buys the 7th tab and costs two dead checkboxes on the Firearm pair.
