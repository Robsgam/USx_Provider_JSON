### B.2 VERIFYING A FOUNDATION / LIVE INSTALL FROM A TENANT EXPORT (method, proven 2026-08-13)

   no top-level `version` field by design ÃƒÆ’Ã†â€™Ãƒâ€šÃ‚Â¢ÃƒÆ’Ã‚Â¢ÃƒÂ¢Ã¢â€šÂ¬Ã…Â¡Ãƒâ€šÃ‚Â¬ÃƒÆ’Ã‚Â¢ÃƒÂ¢Ã¢â‚¬Å¡Ã‚Â¬Ãƒâ€šÃ‚Â the platform rejects a dotted string as `java.lang.Integer`.
