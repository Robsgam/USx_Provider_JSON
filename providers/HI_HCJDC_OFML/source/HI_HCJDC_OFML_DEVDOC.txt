Routing and Configuration Information:
Mnemonic Routing

 The HCJDC assigned ORI for a device (or for the site) should be placed in the
    <Authentication>/<ORI> field in the ConnectCIC header.

 The HCJDC assigned Mnemonic for a device should be placed in the
    <Authentication>/<Mnemonic> field in the ConnectCIC header.

 The User Name should be placed in the <Authentication>/<UserName> field in the
    ConnectCIC header.
