Routing and Configuration Information:
Save byte routing

 The LEMS assigned ORI for a device (or for the site) should be placed in the
    <Authentication>/<ORI> field in the ConnectCIC header.
         <Authentication>/< OriginatingAgencyORI> can be used to override the
         <Authentication>/<ORI>.

 The LEMS assigned Mnemonic (also known as "LDI") for a device should be placed
    in the <Authentication>/<Mnemonic> field in the ConnectCIC header.

 The site-assigned User ID should be placed in the <Authentication>/<UserName>
    field in the ConnectCIC header.
