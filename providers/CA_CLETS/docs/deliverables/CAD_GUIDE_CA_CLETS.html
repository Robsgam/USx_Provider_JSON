Integration Insights

  • ⇨ Name queries fire across multiple entities. CAD sends nameFirst/nameLast for: Vehicle, Person, Firearm, Article, Boat. A subject name on a CAD incident auto-triggers queries on all these entities simultaneously.
  • ⇨ Article: always officer-initiated. CAD does not provide the required fields: ArticleSerialNumber, ownerAppliedNumber. These may use form-specific naming (e.g. DH-suffix fieldIds) or represent data not part of standard CAD dispatch events.
  • ⇨ ArticleSerialNumber not in CAD field list for entity: Article. Combos requiring this field will never auto-trigger from CAD.
  • ⇨ criminalIdNumber not in CAD field list for entity: Person. Combos requiring this field will never auto-trigger from CAD.
  • ⇨ ownerAppliedNumber not in CAD field list for entity: Article, Boat. Combos requiring this field will never auto-trigger from CAD.
