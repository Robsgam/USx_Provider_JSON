  *** THE GATE HARNESS FOUND A REAL DEFECT BY FAILING, AND MY FIRST DIAGNOSIS WAS WRONG. ***
  audit_gate_efficacy reported "codetype-select-as-input -- validate.ps1 *** SURVIVED -- GATE IS BLIND
  TO THIS ***" (7/8 killed). That reads as a tool blind spot, and I spent three probes chasing
  validate.ps1's guard before measuring the mutation's TARGET. The mutation walks
  Vehicle -> Person for relatedHitSearchIndicator, then ImageIndicator, and flips the first hit to
  FormInput. TN's Person relatedHitSearchIndicator WAS ALREADY A FormInput with no codeTypeCategory --
  so the mutation was a NO-OP. Nothing changed, so no gate could react, and the harness reported
  SURVIVED. A FALSE SURVIVED, which usx-tooling warns is worse than a missed one because it sends you
  to widen a gate that already works.
  BUT THE REASON IT WAS A NO-OP IS THE DEFECT: a free-text stolen check where a coded Y/N is required
  means the officer can type anything and it reaches the wire UNCODED -- precisely the class that
  mutation exists to catch, and the same defect AZ_AZDPS fixed ("the tenant showed ... FREE-TEXT
  Stolen Check"). Every other provider carrying this field builds it as a Sel with YES_NO_UNKNOWN|NCIC.
  PROOF THE DIAGNOSIS WAS RIGHT: after converting it, the SAME mutation is now [KILLED] findings 0 -> 1
  and gate efficacy reads 8/8, 0 SURVIVED. validate.ps1 was never blind -- the build was defective, and
  fixing the build restored the mutation's power. Do not "fix" validate.ps1 for this.
  Default 'Y' is safe and follows Rob's stolen-check ruling (OH_LEADS 2026-08-18): the field is in
  any[] on 3 combos and in NO set[] and NO condition, so BUILD_RULES 24 cannot bite. The CAD twin is
  required because CAD ignores the form initialValue.
