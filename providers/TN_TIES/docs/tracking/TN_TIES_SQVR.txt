every entity is split IN-STATE vs OUT-OF-STATE by mutually exclusive `RegistrationState`
EXISTS / NOT_EXISTS conditions. OOS keyRefs are the Nlets ones (RQ.P, RQ.V, DQ.N, DQ.O, KQ.O, BB.*);
in-state keyRefs reach TIES/DMV/NCIC (RQ01, RQ03, RQ05-07, QWA, DQ01, DQ06, DQ05, QB.*).

makes State always-present, every NOT_EXISTS gate permanently false, and EVERY in-state search dead in
one stroke -- LIMITATION #30 / BUILD_RULES 24. The card title carries the routing hint instead.
