Routing and Configuration Information: Save byte routing
 The METERS assigned ORI for a device (or for the site) should be placed in the <Authentication>/<ORI> field in the ConnectCIC header.
 The METERS assigned PID for a device should be placed in the <Authentication>/<Mnemonic> field in the ConnectCIC header.
 The METERS assigned User ID for a user should be placed in the <Authentication>/<UserName> field in the ConnectCIC header.

Security Requirements:
Each site must request METERS access for the vendor and application and complete the necessary paperwork, which will include a network diagram and information about the application's purpose and use. The physical and application security is addressed. This paperwork is necessary to gain approval for METERS connectivity. This paperwork must be completed by the site before METERS will release any documentation or provide any connectivity or assistance to vendors.

Logon Requirements: UserName and Password

Certification Requirements:
When the local agency has determined that their testing is complete and they are ready to cut their new system in production METERS will need the following items.
1) METERS will need an email from the individual in charge of the agency stating that the agency's testing has been completed and the agency is ready to move to production. a. This letter will need to contain the IP address of the agency's connection to METERS (Ed Nabors will be able to provide this). b. The cut over date must also be at least two weeks in advance of the send date of the email. c. All other items which follow must be completed.
