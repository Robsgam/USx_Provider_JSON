v3.4  2026-06-14  Poisoned-array fix (DL+DH ImageIndicator conditions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGED : Removed inert `ImageIndicator EQUALS Y` conditions from all DriverLicenseQuery
          and DriverHistoryQuery combos. Merged the Image-path / catchall combo pairs:
          DL 7 combos -> 4 (DQName, QWName, CPLName, DQOLN); DH 4 -> 2 (KQName, KQOLN).
          image/email/reason now carried in any[] (no longer condition-gated); CAD values
          supplied via combo defaults[] (ImageIndicator=Y, ReasonCode=C, PurposeCode=C, State=TX).
REASON  : Value-comparison conditions (EQUALS/NOT_EQUALS/IN/NOT_IN/REGEX) are wholly inert
          on the platform -- they disable the entire conditions array and never route
          (poisoned-array, QIDM_REFERENCE Sec 2a; live-proven FL v4.9 T-A/T-B). The v3.2
          Img/catchall split therefore over-sent (union of both matching branches).
          Collapsing to single combos with the fields in any[] is the correct pattern.
SCRIPT  : scripts/build_tx_tlets.ps1
OUTPUT  : TX_TLETS.json (6 cards, 22 combos, 6 QIDMs)
NOTE    : EmailAddress remains manual-entry on DL+DH (1 LIM) -- auto-fill handler pending.

v3.2  2026-05-26  Conditional image/email routing (ImageIndicator conditions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGED : Conditions routing on DL + DH QIDMs: ImageIndicator EQUALS Y selects
          image-path combos (with email+image+reason in any[]); catchall combos
          (no conditions) omit those fields entirely.
          Email moved from DL card + DH card to shared OPTIONS card (single field).
          DH EmailAddress sourceField changed from emailAddressDH to emailAddress (shared).
          DL combos: 4 -> 7 (3 image-path + 3 catchall + QW unchanged).
          DH combos: 2 -> 4 (2 image-path + 2 catchall).
          Image-path keyRefs: DQNameImg, CPLNameImg, DQOLNImg, KQNameImg, KQOLNImg.
          Catchall keyRefs: DQName, CPLName, DQOLN, KQName, KQOLN.
          OPTIONS card: 4 fields (State, Image, Reason, Email) in @('3','3','3','3').
REASON  : Officers typed email twice (DL + DH cards). CAD dispatch couldn't fire
          DL/DH because CAD doesn't send email and email was in set[].
          Blank image/reason/email values may cause TX provider failure.
          Conditions routing omits those QIDM attributes when Image != Y.
PENDING : Live platform test needed — verify QW Name combo (no image/email in
          set[]/any[]) confirms platform omits those attributes from XML.
          If platform sends them anyway, pivot to Path B (two QIDMs per query).
