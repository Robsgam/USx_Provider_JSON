v2.1  2026-05-06  PlateType/PlateYear defaults (cross-provider audit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGED : LicensePlateTypeCode initialValue='PC', LicensePlateYear initialValue='2026' (both BASE+MC).
REASON  : Standard defaults per cross-provider audit (6/8 providers already had them).
          Safe because State (not PlateType/Year) is the combo routing discriminator.
          PlateType/PlateYear presence distinguishes RQ from QV in TX, but defaults
          on both means RQ always fires when Plate+Year+PlateType are populated.
          QV (plate-only, no year/type) fires when ONLY plate is filled.
