Routing and Configuration Information: Mnemonic Routing
 The LEDS assigned ORI for a device (or for the site) should be placed in the <Authentication>/<ORI> field in the ConnectCIC header.
 The LEDS assigned Mnemonic for a device should be placed in the <Authentication>/<Mnemonic> field in the ConnectCIC header.
Security Requirements: LEDS logs all computer transactions and inquiries and maintains the logs for two years. At the end of two year cycle, all stored data is destroyed with the exception of criminal history inquiries by SID and/or FBI number which are maintained forever.

Logon Requirements: None known at this time.

Certification Requirements: Agency sponsor is required.
Data-Mined Transactions: NCIC (QA, QB, QG, QV, QW) and DMV (Person and Vehicle) Tags returned from Data mining
