Vehicle -- VehicleRegistrationQuery (3 combos, 8 logs)
  [CONFIRMED] RQ.PO  set=[LicensePlateNumber]
                     any=[LicensePlateTypeCode, LicensePlateYear, RegistrationState]
                     cond: RegistrationState EXISTS                                1 log,  PASS
  [CONFIRMED] RQ.V   set=[VehicleIdentificationNumber] any=[RegistrationState]
                     cond: LicensePlateNumber NOT_EXISTS                           2 logs, PASS
  [CONFIRMED] RQ.P   set=[LicensePlateNumber]
                     any=[LicensePlateTypeCode, LicensePlateYear]
                     cond: RegistrationState NOT_EXISTS                            5 logs, PASS
    THE IN/OUT SPLIT IS THE LOAD-BEARING DETAIL HERE. RQ.PO and RQ.P have IDENTICAL set[]
    ([LicensePlateNumber]); they are separated ONLY by mutually exclusive conditions on
    RegistrationState (EXISTS vs NOT_EXISTS), which is what keeps BOTH reachable. Consequently
    `RegistrationState` MUST NOT be given a form `initialValue` -- a prefill makes State always
    present, RQ.P's NOT_EXISTS gate permanently false, and the in-state plate search DEAD
    (LIMITATION #30 / BUILD_RULES 24). The card title carries the routing hint instead.
    Plate > VIN priority PROVEN by RQ.V's `LicensePlateNumber NOT_EXISTS` gate.
