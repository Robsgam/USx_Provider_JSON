====================================================================================
  HOW THE MATCH IS MADE, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
====================================================================================
  Identity is the bundle ROUTING KEY -- the `provider` field the platform dispatches on to
  reach a state system -- resolved to the provider we would have to support (exact name, then
  unique prefix). It is a fact about the configuration, not a similarity score.

  "IDENTICAL" means the provider bundle is byte-identical after canonicalisation (description
  excluded, platform nulls normalised), so one fix covers the whole family and one defect is
  present on every member.

  IT DOES NOT SAY the config is ours (none carry our version stamp), who wrote it, or that it
  works. Query coverage is shown to indicate how complete each one is RELATIVE to our build
  for the same system -- it is not evidence of authorship or of correctness.
====================================================================================
