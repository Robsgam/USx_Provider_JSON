  CRITICAL FINDING -- set/any controls matching only, not field sending.
    SexCode, BirthDate, and Name are NOT in DQN set[] or any[], yet they were sent.
    All populated attributes in the config are sent when a combination fires,
    regardless of whether they are in set[] or any[] of the matched combination.
    This reverts LIMITATION #10 to the original v1.7 understanding.

  IMPLICATION -- v2.0 correction to LIMITATION #10 was incorrect.
    v2.0 stated: "set/any controls both matching AND sending."
    v2.1 evidence: set/any controls matching only. Field isolation through set/any
    is NOT possible. Field isolation requires separate configs (split attribute lists).

  NOTE -- Extra fields sent with OLN path do not cause rejection.
    ConnectCIC accepted the request with Name/BirthDate/SexCode alongside OLN.
    Server appears to ignore extra fields and use OLN for the DQN query path.
