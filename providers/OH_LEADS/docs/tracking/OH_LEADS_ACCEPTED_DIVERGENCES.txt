HOW THIS WAS ALMOST GOT WRONG, recorded because the reasoning is the valuable part: both authorities appeared to agree (metadata defines the fields on this TRANSACTION, devdoc lists them on this QUERY) and the fix was drafted and written into the build script before the per-COMBINATION <Requirements> were read.
