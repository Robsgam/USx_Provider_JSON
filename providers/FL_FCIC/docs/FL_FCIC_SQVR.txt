  Fields: OLN (DH), Destination State (no default), Purpose Code, Last/First (DH),

  AUTHORITY (FCIC, 2026-06-12): "Since the KQ is out of state and <XX> which

    any: [PurposeCodeDH, AttentionDH]

  KQ+OLN -- Nlets OOS Driver History (OLN) [PENDING]
    keyReference: KQOperatorLicenseNumber
    set: [OperatorLicenseNumberDH, RegistrationStateDH]
    any: [PurposeCodeDH, AttentionDH]

  corrected design (NY DH-OOS pattern).
